SIDE B

magnolia court · nights one & two

Memphis, 1994. You're the new overnight maintenance man at Magnolia Court Apartments. Dwayne, the property manager, pages you jobs from Shoney's.

Reset a breaker. Take out the trash. Deal with the noise complaint about Unit 19, which has been empty since 1991.

Then your pager goes off. It's from Unit 19.

Night two: a cop in the lot, a payphone that rings for the new guy, and a tape with a second side. Two endings. One of them requires running.
